Events and schedule

Primary source is always playriftbound.com organized play. This page is a fan-side digest of August–December 2026.

Just played

  • 14–23 August 2026 — Regional Qualifier Barcelona (2,130+ players). Ornn won. Official recap: Barcelona's Top Decks.
  • 23 August 2026 — NRG Series $5k Constructed Showdown (Vendetta constructed).
  • 30 August 2026 — S4 Wuhan Regional Open (~1,280 players).
  • 4–6 September 2026 — Regional Qualifier Singapore, Singapore EXPO. Akali defeated Kennen in the finals. Official preview: All Eyes on Singapore.

Coming up

  • 23 October 2026 — Radiance (set 5) releases. Enters Standard.
  • 6–8 November 2026 — European Regional Championship, Hall H, Messe Stuttgart. Standard including Radiance. $50,000. Top 8 to Worlds. Official info.
  • 11–13 December 2026 — North American Regional Championship at Convergence Fest, Las Vegas. Standard including Radiance. Official info.
  • Chinese Regional Championship replaces the Radiance Major (256-player invitational plus a large open).

Local ladder (still 2026)

Nexus Nights, Pre-Rift events, Showdown Series, and Summoner Skirmish (through Radiance). For 2027 Riot is retiring Summoner Skirmish in favor of Challenge Series + Nexus Cup, and renaming Regional Qualifiers to Riftbound Regionals. Details: August 2026 State of Play.
