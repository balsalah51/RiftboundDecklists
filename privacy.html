Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Riftbound Decklists ("we," "us," or "this site") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit riftbounddecklists.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Automatically collected information: Like most websites, we automatically collect certain information when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and time spent on the site. This is collected through cookies, log files, and similar technologies.

Information you provide: If you join a Discord later, or contact us directly, any information you share there (such as a username or message) is subject to that platform's own privacy policy, not this one. We do not require account creation or collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details through this site.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how visitors use the site, remember basic preferences, and support advertising if ads are enabled. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

A first-party cookie named rbdb-theme stores only light or dark so the Light / Dark toggle at the top of every page can restore your last choice on the next visit. It lasts up to one year, is not used for advertising or tracking, and clearing cookies returns the site to light mode.

Advertising

This site is built to host display ads once a publisher account is approved. Inventory is reserved for Google AdSense first (auto ads plus labeled units). Carbon Ads is the backup if we want a smaller, TCG-adjacent network. Empty publisher IDs keep the slots hidden so Core Web Vitals stay clean. Opt out of personalized Google ads in Ads Settings. See advertise and affiliates for how to turn ads on.

Affiliate partnerships

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission. That does not change the price you pay.

  • Amazon. We are an Amazon Associate. The Shop links to Amazon for sleeves, dice, playmats, deck boxes, and table extras, and we earn from qualifying purchases. These are the same affiliate listings used on One Piece Deck Base.
  • TCGplayer. We are a TCGplayer affiliate (Impact partner 7670706 / 1780961 / 21018). Buy links on decklists and the price tracker go to TCGplayer, and we may earn a commission if you purchase after clicking them.
  • Cardmarket. Catalog search links open Riftbound singles on Cardmarket (EU). Tracking is added after a partner ID is set in js/ads-config.js.
  • eBay Partner Network. Catalog searches for Riftbound singles. Campaign tracking is added after a campid is set.
  • CardNexus. Optional partner URL for the all-TCG marketplace; empty until approved.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand site traffic and usage patterns. This data is used in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Links

Our site links to third-party content, including tournament results, retailers, and Discord. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

Fair use and not affiliated

Riftbound Decklists is a fan site. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Riot Games, UVS Games, Tencent, or League of Legends. Riftbound, League of Legends, Arcane, and all related names, marks, and distinctive likenesses are property of their owners. Original illustrations on this site are newly created, stylized card-frame portraits for identification — they are not scans of official cards and are not identical to Riot product art.

Tournament lists are republished from public official coverage and public event postings for commentary, reporting, and fan reference. If you are a rights holder and want a list or image taken down, use Discord when the invite is posted, or open an issue on the site repository.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
