Advertise and affiliates

Riftbound Decklists is a high-intent fan archive: people land here to copy a Standard list and buy the cards. That is the inventory we sell — display ads, tracked singles links, and table-gear affiliates. This page is the menu. Nothing loads a third-party ad script until a publisher ID is approved.

Display ads (hosting)

Every page has labeled top and bottom units. They stay hidden until js/ads-config.js has enabled: true and an AdSense client. That keeps Core Web Vitals clean while the site is in review.

Google AdSense

Best first network for a custom-domain GitHub Pages site. After approval:

  1. Put ca-pub-… in ADSENSE_PUB in scripts/build.py and in js/ads-config.js (adsenseClient, enabled: true).
  2. Rebuild. ads.txt gets the official google.com, pub-…, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0 line. Slots unhide. Auto ads are optional.
  3. Confirm /ads.txt returns HTTP 200 on the root domain.

Carbon Ads

Smaller, design-forward units. Fit a TCG fan site better than a wall of remnant display. Add carbonServe and carbonPlacement in the same config when you have a campaign.

Mediavine, Raptive, Playwire, Ezoic

Session-gated premium networks. Apply when traffic supports it. Do not load their scripts until a contract exists — they will tank LCP if they sit idle.

Direct sponsorships

Sleeve, playmat, dice, and local-shop brands can buy a labeled homepage or legend-hub placement. That is usually worth more than remnant AdSense on a niche TCG URL. Discord is the contact placeholder until an invite is posted.

Affiliate programs

Live today

  • Amazon Associates — Shop sleeves, dice, playmats, deck boxes, table extras. Same listings as One Piece Deck Base.
  • TCGplayer (Impact) — Partner 7670706 / 1780961 / 21018. Copy-list and per-card Buy buttons, plus mass entry. Highest intent on this site.

Wired, waiting on IDs

  • Cardmarket — EU Riftbound singles catalog. Search links are already on deck pages. Add cardmarketId in js/ads-config.js to track.
  • eBay Partner Network — Secondary-market singles and sealed. Set ebayCampId to attach campaign parameters.
  • CardNexus — All-TCG marketplace partner program. Set cardnexusUrl to a tracked landing page.

Worth applying

  • Card Kingdom / Channel Fireball / CoolStuffInc store affiliates if they add Riftbound SKUs.
  • Dragon Shield / Ultra Pro brand programs for sleeves and boxes (Amazon already covers many of those SKUs).
  • Riot / UVS creator activations are contract-by-set, not an automated affiliate network. Do not claim an official Riftbound partner badge.

FTC: affiliate links are marked rel="sponsored". Amazon and TCGplayer disclosures sit on shop, prices, and deck pages.

FAQ

Are ads on the site right now?

Slots exist. They do not request ad servers until a publisher ID is enabled. You should not see empty “Advertisement” boxes in the meantime.

Will ads change list copy?

No. Tournament lists stay the product. Ads sit above and below the article, labeled, and are skipped by crawlers with data-nosnippet.
