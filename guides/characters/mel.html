Mel, Soul's Reflection

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Chaos / Mind

Game plan

Chaos-Mind council mage. Time Warp piles that Best-Of'd Barcelona. You play the long game with extra turns and extra spells.

Key cards

Time Warp, Mind draw, Chaos tricks, council payoffs.

Matchups

Outlasts Nasus. Loses to anything that scores on turn four. Keep this for rooms that will let you warp.

When to register it

C-tier combo-adjacent. Do not take it to a Showdown full of Rengar.
