Ezreal, Prodigal Explorer

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Chaos / Mind

Game plan

Chaos-Mind spells. Singapore Top 16s on attrition. You draw, recycle, and never present a battlefield the way Yi does.

Key cards

Prodigy / explorer package, Stacked Deck, Time Warp in some lists, cheap cantrips, Chaos/Mind runes.

Matchups

Outlasts fair Ornn. Explodes to Rengar. Can steal Game 1 vs Kennen if you have the counter suite.

When to register it

A B-tier legend for players who want Diana's domains with more instant-speed cards.
