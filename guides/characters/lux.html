Lux, Lady of Luminosity

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Mind / Order

Game plan

Mind-Order light. Singapore playmat promo and a fringe Day 2 legend. Control, counters, one big spell.

Key cards

Crownguard / luminosity champion, counters, Order recycle, Mind draw.

Matchups

Can brick Kennen if the counter suite is real. Cannot beat a battlefield race. Keep it as a local control option.

When to register it

C-tier control. Play it if you refuse to play Ezreal and still want Mind-Order.
