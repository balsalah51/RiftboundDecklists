Zed, Master of Shadows

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Chaos / Fury

Game plan

Chaos-Fury clones. Still Best-Ofs in Vendetta Standard. You copy, you shadow, you try to be a combat Kennen.

Key cards

Master of Shadows champion, clone units, Chaos tricks, Fury combat.

Matchups

Worse Kennen with extra clones. Sometimes that is enough to Best-Of. Rarely enough to Top 8.

When to register it

D-tier shadows.
