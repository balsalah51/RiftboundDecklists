FAQ

What is Riftbound Decklists?

A fan archive of public Riftbound TCG Standard constructed lists. Browse by Legend, copy a tournament list, and read current-meta strategy. Not affiliated with Riot Games or UVS Games.

What format are these lists?

Standard. That is the only constructed legality in 2026 organized play. Deck construction notes are on the format page.

What is a Legend?

The identity card that sits outside your 40. This site organizes decks by Legend. Strategy for each name is on the legend hub and in legend strategy.

How is Vendetta meta share counted?

The pie on this page only includes lists that run at least one Vendetta (VEN) card. Origins-only piles are left out. Percentages are of that filtered pool, grouped by Legend, and the key under the chart also shows each legend's tier-list score.

How do I buy a list?

Copy list, then Buy on TCGplayer (Impact affiliate). Table gear in the shop uses Amazon Associate links. Cardmarket and eBay catalog links sit next to buy buttons for EU and secondary-market singles.
