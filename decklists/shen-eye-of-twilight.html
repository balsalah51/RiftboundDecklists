Calm-Order Kinkou tank. A Best-Of legend that lives on holds. You stall, you shield, you hope they cannot storm over the temple.

Kinkou champion, Disciple of Shen, Ki Barrier, Scuttle, Sona, Calm/Order runes. Leader of the Kinkou Order is a unit name, not a format term.
