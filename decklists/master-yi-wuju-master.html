Game plan

Origins Yi. Same domains, worse payoff in Vendetta. Barcelona still handed it a Best-Of, but Top 8s belong to Bladesman.

Key cards

Tempered or Honed depending on the list, Whiteflame Protector, Dazzling Aurora in older builds. Modern tables should just play Bladesman cards.

Matchups

You are a step behind every Bladesman list in the room. Treat this as a collection constraint, not a meta call.

When to register it

Only if Bladesman is unavailable. Convert to the Unleashed legend as soon as you can.
