Fiora, Grand Duelist

Body / Order Unleashed Standard

Body-Order duelist. Quiet at Barcelona, then a Singapore Top 8. You win single combat, not wide turns.

Full Fiora strategy →

Current meta

August–September 2026

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Body / Order

Game plan

Body-Order duelist. Quiet at Barcelona, then a Singapore Top 8. You win single combat, not wide turns.

Key cards

Worthy / Victorious champion, Punch First, Challenge, En Garde, combat gear. Often shares Body cards with Yi and Sett.

Matchups

Preferred into one-threat Kennen draws. Punished by go-wide Azir and by Akali that never duels fair. Wuhan put Fiora in Top 4 in a value room.

When to register it

A specialist anti-hero. Register it when you expect legends that present one unit at a time.
