Current meta

August–September 2026

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Chaos / Mind

Game plan

Chaos-Mind moonfall. Former regional winner that still Best-Ofs. Auckland 10K had three Dianas in the Top 10. You play spells, flip the battlefield, and grind.

Key cards

Lunari champion, Tideturner, Hwei, Vex, Apathetic, Stupefy, Time Warp in greedy lists, Chaos/Mind runes.

Matchups

Fine into Kennen if you have discard and counter. Worse against Rengar speed. Conversion from Day 1 to Top 64 was real in Wuhan.

When to register it

The spell-based alternative to Ezreal in the same domains. Take Diana if you want more unit interaction and less pure draw-go.
