Vex, Gloomist

Calm / Chaos Unleashed Standard

Calm-Chaos gloom. Conversion is real in stacked-deck rooms because you tax their hand and their cheer.

Full Vex strategy →

Current meta

August–September 2026

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Calm / Chaos

Game plan

Calm-Chaos gloom. Conversion is real in stacked-deck rooms because you tax their hand and their cheer.

Key cards

Apathetic / Cheerless, gloom spells, Tideturner overlap with Irelia, cheap Calm.

Matchups

Better against Kennen than the C-tier label suggests if you draw the tax pieces. Worse against combat-first Yi.

When to register it

A side-strategy legend. Register it when you have a read that the room is 40% storm.
