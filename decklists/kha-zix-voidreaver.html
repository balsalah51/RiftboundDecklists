Current meta

August–September 2026

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Body / Chaos

Game plan

Body-Chaos evolve. Singapore Top 16 and a Barcelona Best-Of. Isolate, grow, score.

Key cards

Mutating Horror, Traveling Merchant, Treasure Hunter, Fizz, Void Assault, Invert Timelines in greedy lists.

Matchups

Good against legends that present one unit. Bad against Azir wide and against Kennen that never 1v1s you. Keep isolation spells.

When to register it

A B-tier spicy Body splash if you are bored of Yi and Rengar.
