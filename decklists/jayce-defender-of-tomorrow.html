Current meta

August–September 2026

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Body / Mind

Game plan

Body-Mind inventor ramp. Singapore's promo legend and a real Day 2 option. You accelerate into bombs and try not to die to Lightning Rush.

Key cards

Brilliant Inventor, Dazzling Aurora / Platewyrm lines in some lists, Mobilize, Catalyst of Aeons, Elder Dragon in the greedy versions. NRG Milwaukee posted several Jayce Day 2s.

Matchups

Wins long games vs Ornn-fair. Loses to Kennen on the play if your ramp skip is slow. Incomplete lists are common — copy a complete 62–66 card submission, not a placeholder.

When to register it

Play Jayce if you like ramp and the room is full of midrange, not storm.
