Current meta

August–September 2026

Vendetta Standard · August–September 2026 · Fury / Calm

Game plan

Singapore Regional Qualifier winner. Fury-Calm assassin that snowballs openings instead of racing Kennen on raw storm. The winning lists kept the SFD cheap units and added a Vendetta 7-drop plus Origins Blitzcrank, Impassive as the disruption package.

Key cards

Cheap empowered 2-drops, Silent / Deadly Weapon champions, VEN-028 and VEN-044 style hold units, OGN-067 Blitzcrank in the Singapore finalist shell. Punch First and combat tricks close the score.

Matchups

Built to beat Kennen in a long final. Worse against go-wide Azir and against Yi that sticks Trophy Hunter first. Side extra removal for Discord and extra units for the Irelia grind.

When to register it

Register Akali if the room is 30%+ Kennen and you are comfortable playing from behind for a turn. This is the best 'I am not playing the bogeyman' legend in September 2026.
